Tyros Academy

Building Strategic Client Relationships.

Move from one-off supplier to trusted partner.

Updated October 2026

Who it is for

Consultants, account managers, business developers, partners, senior recruiters and commercial leaders.

Objectives

  • Understand how an account is organised and where decisions are made.
  • Build several relationships inside the same group.
  • Turn an assignment into a long-term relationship.

Programme

  • Identify sponsors, users and decision-makers.
  • Bring value before selling.
  • Manage the relationship after the first assignment.
  • Relevant cross-selling.
  • Obtain internal introductions.
  • Protect the client relationship.

The Tyros approach

Designed for financial services, where a relationship is built assignment after assignment. It draws on hands-on experience of client relationships in financial services.

Practitioner-led and grounded in hands-on experience in specialist recruitment, business development and client relationships within financial services.

Format

Formats available from a 60 to 90 minute executive session to a half-day or full-day workshop, depending on the programme and audience.

  • In person or remote, in French or English.
  • Practical cases and exercises; knowledge check where relevant.
  • Attestation of participation. It is not an official certification.
