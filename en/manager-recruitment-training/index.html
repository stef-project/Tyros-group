Manager as Recruiter: Interviewing & Selecting Specialist Talent.

For managers who take part in hiring without being professional recruiters.

Updated October 2026

Who it is for

Hiring Managers, Heads of Department, managers and leaders of specialist teams.

Objectives

  • Run a structured interview that is comparable from one candidate to the next.
  • Tell real expertise from a rehearsed pitch.
  • Avoid the process mistakes that lose the best candidates.

Programme

  • Prepare a structured interview.
  • Ask questions that genuinely reveal a candidate's level.
  • Assess motivation, potential and flight risk.
  • Understand notice periods, counter-offers and financial motivations.
  • Assess transferable skills.
  • Sell the company to the candidate as well.
  • Give effective feedback.

The Tyros approach

Cases and examples drawn from Finance, Risk, Compliance, Audit, Actuarial and other specialist functions. This is training in interview practice, not psychology or coaching.

Practitioner-led and grounded in hands-on experience in specialist recruitment, business development and client relationships within financial services.

Format

Formats available from a 60 to 90 minute executive session to a half-day or full-day workshop, depending on the programme and audience.

  • In person or remote, in French or English.
  • Practical cases and exercises; knowledge check where relevant.
  • Attestation of participation. It is not an official certification.
