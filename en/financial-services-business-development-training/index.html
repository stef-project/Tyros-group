Tyros Academy

Business Development in Financial Services.

From Cold Prospect to Trusted Client. An approach built for complex, regulated B2B environments, not for retail selling.

Updated October 2026

Who it is for

Business Developers, consultants, recruiters, account managers, Sales Leaders and B2B entrepreneurs.

Objectives

  • Open an account with no prior relationship.
  • Turn a conversation into an opportunity, across long cycles.
  • Grow an account after the first assignment.

Programme

  • Map a market and select priority accounts.
  • Identify the right decision-makers.
  • Email, LinkedIn and phone outreach; securing a first meeting.
  • Understand long B2B cycles and follow up intelligently.
  • Build several sponsors inside one account.
  • Use a CRM properly.
  • Generate introductions and referrals.

The Tyros approach

Built for banks, insurers and consulting firms, where decision-makers are scarce, in demand and cautious. Examples come from specialist recruitment and large-account development in financial services.

Practitioner-led and grounded in hands-on experience in specialist recruitment, business development and client relationships within financial services.

Format

Formats available from a 60 to 90 minute executive session to a half-day or full-day workshop, depending on the programme and audience.

  • In person or remote, in French or English.
  • Practical cases and exercises; knowledge check where relevant.
  • Attestation of participation. It is not an official certification.
